## 2. MEDIUM — "Keep the old 98 binding-ID set/5460 bytes intact" is mechanically impossible

**Location.** Plan line 86. Source: `read-update-matrix.test.ts:5028–5038`, `:4949–4968`.

**Issue.** `additional` is **derived live**, not frozen:

```

  .flatMap(a => a.assertions.map(q => a.id + "." + q.id));
expect(additional).toHaveLength(98);
```

`addedIds` is pinned at 16 entries (`:4967`) and **includes `nr-bead-schema-read` and `nr-link-schema-read`** (`:4961`, `:4965`) — consistent with plan line 102's "the converted four actions belong to the previous 16-added set." There is no authoring choice that keeps 98/5460 while adding `written`, short of removing the two `-read` actions from `addedIds`, which would push them into `unchanged` and break the 48-action list and its `3378857a…` digest.

Independently confirmed: the plan's own `3302` figure is the byte sum of **60** bindings including the two `written` (I reproduce 58 → 3216, +2 written → 86, total 3302), so the plan already counts them on the *new* side while line 86 also asserts they are excluded from a frozen old side that cannot exclude them.

**Concrete fix.** Restate line 86 as the split the guard actually produces: old `addedIds` ledger becomes **100 bindings / 5546 bytes** (98/5460 plus the two `written`), new-action bindings are **58 / 3216 bytes**, total subtrahend 8762, remainder 131 ≤ 1708.
