### 6. Unknown-key batches normalize every operation before the operation-count refusal
**Severity: low (efficiency)**
**File:** `packages/server/src/transaction-store.ts:520` vs `:529`

`prepareTransaction` no longer caps operation count (`transaction-batch.ts:84-89`; I verified 512 operations prepare cleanly — correct, since the spec makes the count an *admission control*, not carrier syntax). But `normalizeTransaction` runs over every operation — identity, witnesses, and per-field `resolveAlias` SQLite lookups — *before* `controls.maximumOperations` refuses. A 64 KiB batch of alias-spelled `updateBead` members does hundreds of point lookups inside the atomic owner step and is then refused with `operation-limit`.

**Impact.** Bounded by the 64 KiB body cap, private-only, no durable effect (no receipt or owner is inserted).
**Fix.** Move the count check above normalization on the `!prior` path only; it must stay below it for the prior-key equality/conflict comparison, which the spec orders first.
