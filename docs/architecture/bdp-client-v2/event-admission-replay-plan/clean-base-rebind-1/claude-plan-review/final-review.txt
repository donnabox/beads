## Findings

Smallest correction: specify the new intent as a **separate disjunct** leaving 1783-1788 byte-identical, e.g. `admission === "current-event" && (source.kind === "bead" || source.kind === "link" || (source.kind === "scope" && source.url === new URL("events/", scope).href))`, with **no** `after` and **no** `!invalidReconnect` conjunct, selecting only `snapshotCurrentReadMinimum` + `checkCurrentReadMinimum(..., true)`.
