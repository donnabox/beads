**1. Medium — frozen source binding omits dependencies the plan's own correctness arguments rest on**
- Location: `transaction-changefeed-parameter-context-source-binding.json` `files`; plan §"Exactly six owned paths" ("The source binding includes these dependencies and receiving/emission fixtures").
- Issue: the binding hashes several direct imports of the owned files (`transaction-events.ts`, `read-http.ts`, `read-http-routing.ts`, `transaction-service.ts`) but omits others of equal standing: `packages/server/src/transaction-consistency.ts` (defines `TransactionMinimumFailure(reason, context)` at :56–63 and `waitForTransactionMinimum` at :85–121 — the basis for the plan's "epoch/view checks before future-head diagnosis", the preserved `cursor-expired`/`foreign-view` distinction, and the "service propagates, needs no edit" claim), `packages/conformance/src/http-executor.ts` (its `isSecretHeaderName`/`RESERVED_FIELDS` admission at :337–358,:674–675 and frozen `routes` membership at :679 are what make the installed `last-event-id` rows and the post-response-2 derived URL authorable at all), `packages/conformance/test-support/transactional/fixture-v1.json`, `apps/bdptest/test-support/installed-contract-identity.ts`, and `packages/server/src/transaction-batch.ts`. Both of the first two are supplied as ACTUAL FULL SOURCE in this packet — i.e. the packet treats them as load-bearing evidence while the frozen binding does not pin them.
- Impact: the binding is the instrument for the post-merge reanchor. A change to the minimum-failure shape, the executor's header/route admission, or the reused installed fixture would silently invalidate the plan's compatibility and authorability claims without tripping binding verification, and the quoted completeness sentence is an overclaim.
- Minimal fix: add those hashes, or state explicitly that `files` is a curated subset and name the inclusion rule so omissions are not read as absence of dependency.

**5. Low — rows 7/8 depend on client transmission of `Last-Event-ID`, with no adjudication rule for the alternative**
- Location: plan §"Exact source selection" item 4 ("valid URL + malformed selected-SSE Last-Event-ID GET/HEAD"); ledger source requests 7–8; `transaction-http.ts:457–465`.
- Issue: those rows reach the owner only if the callback-local `fetch` actually puts `last-event-id` on the wire. Nothing in the supplied existing source demonstrates that: `transaction-changefeed.test.ts:610–616` resolves to media `json` (so the field is ignored by design at :465), `:617–621` is a `changes/` request that throws at `:99` regardless of the field, and `:622–623` again resolves to `json`. If the field were dropped, row 7 becomes `effective = {after:"genesis"}` → a 200 SSE selection rather than a 400 — a loud failure, correctly, but one whose cause is the client stack, not context propagation.
- Impact: a transport-level negative would be adjudicated as a context-propagation defect, and in the control it would also perturb the "exactly seven named tuple mismatches, twelve completed responses" gate.
- Minimal fix: one adjudication clause — a 200/SSE outcome on rows 7/8 is classified as "selected reconnect field not transmitted by the client stack", not as a context failure. No extra requests needed.

**6. Low — blanket reader cap has no stated behavior for an absent response body**
- Location: plan §"Exact source selection" item 4 transport paragraph ("streaming32768B response cap before retention, fatal UTF-8 decoding and awaited reader cleanup/cancel").
- Issue: the rule is written as if every one of the twelve exchanges has a readable body; rows 4, 6 and 8 are HEAD, whose body is empty or null depending on the fetch implementation. An unguarded `getReader()` on a null body is a hard throw inside the callback (and inside the control run).
- Impact: a startup/transport crash on a prerequisite row, not an oracle result; the control's "twelve completed responses" gate would fail for an unrelated reason.
- Minimal fix: one clause — HEAD/absent-body responses skip the reader and assert zero octets plus the GET-matching `Content-Length` (already required by the HEAD oracle).

**7. Low (unverified from supplied evidence, not a demonstrated defect) — Markdown path in the `biome format` gate**
- Location: ledger `staticCommands[3]`, final argument `docs/design/transaction-implementation-progress.md`, under "sequential stop-first-failure".
- Issue: no Biome version or configuration is in the supplied evidence, so whether an explicitly passed unsupported-language path yields a nonzero diagnostic (rather than a silent skip) cannot be determined here. Under stop-first-failure that distinction gates every later command.
- Impact: possible gate abort unrelated to the change, or a silently unformatted doc.
- Minimal fix: confirm against the pinned Biome config; if explicit unsupported paths diagnose, drop the `.md` from that argv or pass the ignore-unknown flag.
