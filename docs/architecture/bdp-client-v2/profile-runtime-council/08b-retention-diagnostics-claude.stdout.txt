## A. Proven defects (establishable from the supplied packet alone)

### A1 — Medium — All nine reopen assertions are non-falsifiable
**Where:** `apps/bdptest/test-support/development-process-actions.ts:70-80` together with `packages/conformance/development/read-update-manifest-v1.json`, actions `before-expiry`, `at-expiry`, `durable-reopen` (appended block, new-file line ≈12326+).

**Issue:** The action throws before returning unless `exit.code === 0 && exit.signal === null` (lines 74-75), and it returns `elapsedMs` as a verbatim echo of the manifest's own `input.elapsedMs` (line 70 → line 80). The manifest then asserts exactly those three facts per reopen (`/elapsedMs`, `/priorExit/code === 0`, `/priorExit/signal === null`). Reaching the assertion phase already implies all three.

**Trigger:** Any run. A dirty child exit (e.g. `process.exit(2)` from the rejected-close path, `development-process.ts:64`) surfaces as `executionError: {category: "execution"}`, never as an assertion failure.

**Impact:** The lifecycle clean-exit/deadline evidence the packet claims is harness self-attestation, not manifest-observed. 9 of the scenario's assertions contribute to the advertised "302 HTTP/lifecycle actions" surface while being incapable of failing.

**Fix/next step:** Pick one oracle. Either drop the `exit.code/signal` throw at lines 74-75 and let the manifest assertion be the gate (preferred — it makes the dirty-exit case a reported assertion failure), or keep the throw and stop presenting those three as observational assertions.

### A2 — Medium — A spec-`MAY` behavior is asserted with no manifest-level gate
**Where:** manifest actions `forgotten-failure-executes`, `retained-new-delete`, `subject-deleted`, `durable-retained-delete`, `durable-subject-absent`; scenario `applicability.requires: []`.

**Issue:** `docs/specs/bdp.md:3324-3326` is permissive: "after the interval the authority **MAY** forget it entirely." The manifest asserts the forgetting branch as a required observation (`status 200`, `outcome "deleted"`). The "permitted, not required" qualifier exists only in prose — `README.md` ("Failure forgetting is this store's permitted behavior, not a requirement…") and `read-update-coverage-v1.json` row `read-update.idempotency.expired-failure` `limits`. Nothing in the manifest or fixture carries it. `applicability.requires` is empty, while `setup.requires` is used elsewhere in this very scenario to gate on `development-retention-process`, so the gating mechanism exists and is not used here.

**Trigger:** Running this manifest against any authority that retains failed dispositions past the interval (spec-compliant) → spurious fail on `forgotten-failure-executes` and every downstream action.

**Impact:** A permitted-behavior observation is indistinguishable from a required one at the artifact level. Today this is contained by the `kind: diagnostic` lane, but the containment is prose, not structure.

**Fix/next step:** Gate it — e.g. an `applicability.requires` entry naming the forgetting behavior, backed by a fixture capability, mirroring the `development-retention-process` pattern already in `read-update-fixture-v1.json`. If you prefer not to add a capability token, state the permission inside the manifest action rather than only in README/coverage.

### A3 — Medium — Unvalidated injected clock reaches a durable destructive operation
**Where:** `packages/server/src/development-authority.ts:74-80`, `:105`.

**Issue:** `openDevelopmentAuthority` is exported and accepts `clock?: () => number` with no validation, then calls `store.expire(clock())` at line 105. **Trigger:** `openDevelopmentAuthority({ …, clock: () => Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER })`, or any non-monotonic / NaN clock. **Impact:** A single open discards every retained disposition the configured 24 h minimum (`:86`, `:130-131`) would otherwise protect, in a durable store, before `assertReadUpdateRuntimeCompatibility` even runs (`:106-107`). Whether tombstones survive that call is not establishable here (see Q2).

**Baseline framing:** the `scheduler` seam at `:72-73` is the identical pattern and is pre-existing, so the *seam* is baseline. What is new is that the injected value now drives a destructive durable write rather than only reordering turns. "Private" is currently a doc comment (`:74`), not an enforced constraint.

**Fix/next step:** Validate at the authority layer before line 105 — safe integer, non-negative, and non-decreasing across calls within one authority — or keep the symbol off the package's public `exports` map and assert that in `scripts/smoke-executables.mjs` alongside the dist-file list.

## C. Checks requested that came back clean on this packet

Stated plainly, not as praise — these are the items the brief named, with the packet-bounded result.

- **No weakening of production 24 h defaults.** `day = 86_400_000` (`development-authority.ts:35`), `openRecoveryStore({ minimumRetentionMs: day })` (`:86`), and member `retentionMs: day, minimumRetentionMs: day` (`:130-131`) are untouched by the diff.
