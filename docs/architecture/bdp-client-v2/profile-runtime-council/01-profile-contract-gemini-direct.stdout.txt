### 1. Proven Findings

#### Finding 1: Discovery profile membership text and table contradict optional Historical resolution across lower profiles
- **Severity**: Medium
- **File and exact line/section**: [`docs/specs/bdp.md`](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L3720-L3744) lines 3720–3744 (Section: "Discovery profile membership")
- **Concrete issue**: 
  1. Line 3720 asserts: *"The history, receipt, snapshot, changefeed, and Event members are required only in Transactional and prohibited in both lower profiles."* By using the unqualified term *"The history... members"*, this text directly contradicts Section "Status and conformance" ([lines 100–102](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L100-L102)), Section "Scope discovery" ([lines 1088–1090](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L1088-L1090)), Section "Historical resolution" ([lines 1580–1582](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L1580-L1582)), and Section "Read+Update discovery" ([lines 2004–2006](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L2004-L2006)), which establish that Historical resolution (`historicalResolution`) is an optional complete capability admitted on Read and inherited by Read+Update. The context in [lines 3704–3708](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L3704-L3708) indicates that line 3720 intended to refer to Part III Scope history fences (`scopeEpoch`, `authorizationView`, `headPosition`, `minimumReplayPosition`), not the `historicalResolution` capability.
  2. In the summary table at lines 3734–3744, `historicalResolution` is completely omitted from the profile membership rows, even though all other optional members (`limits`, `maximumEndpointMultiplicity`, `order`, and conditional `aliases`) are cataloged.
- **Reachable trigger**: A client, server implementer, or conformance validator relying on Section "Discovery profile membership" to construct or validate a Read or Read+Update discovery document containing `historicalResolution: { "version": 1 }`.
- **Impact**: Potential false-negative rejection of valid Read or Read+Update discovery documents that advertise the optional History capability, or confusion between Part III Scope history fences and Part I Historical resolution.
- **Fix/Next step**:
  1. Clarify line 3720 to state explicitly: *"The Scope history (`scopeEpoch`, `authorizationView`, `headPosition`, `minimumReplayPosition`), receipt, snapshot, changefeed, and Event members are required only in Transactional and prohibited in both lower profiles."*
  2. Add an explicit row to the table at line 3744:
     ```markdown
     | `historicalResolution` | optional | optional | optional |
     ```
- **Baseline vs Regression**: In [`docs/design/spec-present-contract-map.md`](file:///docs/design/spec-present-contract-map.md#L14-L15) lines 14–15, historical definitions were consolidated into Section "Historical resolution" during the editorial pass; the wording in line 3720 and the omission in the profile membership table represent an editorial defect introduced during consolidation.

### 2. Questions Needing Omitted Context

#### Question 1: Schema admission of `patch` and `sequence` limit groups in `transactionalAdvertisedLimits`
- **Severity**: Low
- **File and exact line/section**: [`docs/specs/bdp.md`](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L3775-L3782) lines 3775–3782 (Section: "Transactional limits")
- **Concrete issue**: [Lines 3770–3772](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L3770-L3772) explicitly state that `readUpdateAdvertisedLimits` restates the `page`, `request`, `resource`, `selector`, `patch`, and `sequence` groups unchanged from `advertisedLimits`. In contrast, [lines 3775–3782](file:///docs/specs/bdp.md#L3775-L3782) describe `transactionalAdvertisedLimits` as a closed definition admitting `transaction`, `validation`, `retention.receipt`, `retention.maximumSnapshotLifetime`, and `retention.replay`, and rejecting `retention.idempotency`, but omit mention of whether `patch` and `sequence` groups are retained or rejected.
- **Reachable trigger**: A Transactional authority advertising `patch` or `sequence` limits in its discovery document.
- **Impact**: If `schemas/bdp-v0.schema.json` under `$defs/transactionalAdvertisedLimits` closed the object without including `patch` and `sequence`, advertising inherited mutation limits would cause a schema validation failure.
- **Fix/Next step**: Verify the closed definition of `transactionalAdvertisedLimits` in `schemas/bdp-v0.schema.json` (omitted from this packet) to ensure `patch` and `sequence` are admitted, and align the prose in lines 3775–3782.
- **Baseline vs Regression**: Cannot be determined solely from the provided packet excerpts; schema artifact is omitted.
