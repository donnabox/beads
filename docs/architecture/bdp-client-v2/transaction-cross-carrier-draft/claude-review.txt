## Optional improvements

**3. [Medium] Four "absence" oracles on the two sequence actions cannot fail for any protocol-legal response.**
*Location:* `manifest-v1.json:20424` (`no-receipt`), `:20430` (`no-root`), `:20436` (`no-problem`) in `x-create-sequence`; `:20940/:20946/:20952/:20971` in `x-two-sequence`.
*Issue:* `no-problem` / `no-update-problem` assert `/results/N/problem` is absent. `transaction-sequence.ts:36–63` spreads member problems **flat** into the member, and `#/$defs/transactionalSequenceMemberProblem` has no nested `problem` member — a nested shape is not representable. `no-receipt` / `no-root` are entailed by the `schema` assertion, since `transactionalSequenceResponse` is `additionalProperties:false` with only `results`.
*Impact:* These read as discriminating but never can be. The `sequence-fresh-key` control proves it: the member *is* an identity-taken Problem and `no-problem` is correctly listed in `expectedPassingIds`. Real discrimination rests entirely on `presentation` and `created-record`.
*Correction:* Replace `no-problem` / `no-update-problem` with the reachable negative the control actually needs — `/results/0/code` `exists:false` (and `/results/1/code` for the two-member action) — or keep them and record in the coverage reason that they are structural, not behavioural. Either change re-opens every measured byte/node/hash value, so it is a post-freeze remeasurement, not an edit to this draft.

**4. [Medium] `assertCrossCarrierControls()` never loads either derivative through the bundle, and emits no preflight ledger.**
*Location:* `apps/bdptest/src/transactional-matrix.test.ts:2004–2043`.
*Issue:* The deletion preflight (`:1632–1655`) calls `createCumulativeTransactionalArtifactBundle` on each derivative, asserts three distinct `executionManifestDigest`s, and can write a ledger under `BDP_TRANSACTIONAL_DELETION_PREFLIGHT_OUTPUT`. The cross-carrier preflight does neither.
*Impact:* A structurally inadmissible control (bad assertion `kind`, unresolvable `#/$defs/…` — the controls introduce `#/$defs/transactionalProblem` to this manifest for the first time) would only surface during a cohort run, after child launch. Root also has no app-emitted derivative ledger and must rely on `measure.py` artifacts.
*Correction:* Mirror the deletion preflight: add the bundle load, a distinct-digest assertion, and the optional output env var.

**5. [Medium] The control description's `retained` / `removed` fields are self-derived, so they pin nothing independently.**
*Location:* `transactional-matrix.test.ts:1977` (`const retained = assertions.map(a => a.id)`), consumed at `:2013–2017`.
*Issue:* `retained` is read back out of the manifest and then compared to the manifest; `removed` is a hard-coded `[]` compared to `[]`. Contrast `applyDeletionControl`, which pins an independent `stableIds` list and computes `removed` by diff.
*Impact:* Limited — `for (const id of description.failingIds) expect(description.retained).toContain(id)` still catches loss of any of the 13 / 2 failing IDs, and the execution test's `find(id)?.passed` catches a missing positive. But the non-failing canonical IDs on `x-create-batch` / `x-create-sequence` are pinned only by the row hash in the *other* package's test, and the published control ledger would silently change meaning.
*Correction:* Pin both control actions' canonical assertion-ID lists literally here, as the deletion guard does.

**6. [Low] Presence-before-equality has no named-cause guard for the new row.**
