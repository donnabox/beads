# Blockers

## B1 · The dynamic receipt mechanism is specified two incompatible ways at once — Blocker (H1b authoring)

**Location:** plan §Mandatory dynamic receipt action items 1, 5, 7 · vs `packages/conformance/test-support/transactional/manifest-v1.json` (scenario `transactional.receipt.readable`, action `retained`) · `apps/bdptest/test-support/read-update-exact-client.ts:118-173` · `packages/conformance/src/runner.ts:840-852`, `:855-872`, `:181-190` · `scenario-action.ts:8`, `:55-64` · `executable-manifest.ts:744-789`.

Item 1 demands a new non-`http` family; items 5 and 7 demand the evidence (`dynamicExactConfiguration`, `dynamicExactConfigurationDigest`, `sourceReceiptObservation`, physical binding) be carried by **the private runner result**. Neither matches the only two mechanisms that exist:

- **Caller lane (what RU does and what H1a authored):** `family:"client"` → `runProgrammaticAction` → `options.actionExecutor`. `createInstalledExactClient` already validates a captured URL against canonical Scope/origin/path, rejects userinfo/query/fragment and non-canonical spellings, snapshots a **single-route** exact mode (`{...mode, routes:[route]}`), dials through a fresh raw executor on the same `dialRoute`, and requires `response.exactRequest` to exist (`read-update-exact-client.ts:57-68`, `:118-143`). But it returns a caller-built `{request, route, observation, status, headers, body}` object, and the runner records only `{id, family, operation}` + `output:{kind:"json"}` + assertion outcomes (`runner.ts:181-190`). The dynamic route, its digest, the source linkage and the wire evidence never enter the report — so items 5 and 7 are unimplementable on this lane, and item 6's "not a reserialized synthetic HTTP reply" is violated in letter.
- **Runner lane:** `runProgrammaticAction` receives neither `options` nor `exchanges`, so it cannot reach `exactExecution`, cannot snapshot a route, cannot dial, cannot record an exchange. A new family additionally requires editing the published parser vocabulary (`executable-manifest.ts:774`, error text "must be 'http', 'client', or 'lifecycle'"), the published `ScenarioActionFamily` (`scenario-action.ts:8`), and H1a's manifest action — which contradicts the plan's own "No public manifest/normative schema action is added" and "Update only private source-only TX fixture/manifest/report validation for this action".

**Impact:** implementation cannot start. Either the mandated recorder fields don't exist, or the plan's public-surface and H1a-ownership constraints break.

**Fix (recommended, and it is cheap):** keep H1a's encoding and move the *dial* into the private runner. In `runMatrix(..., transactional=true)` only, intercept `family:"client" && operation==="transactional.receipt-read"` in `runScenarioAction` (`runner.ts:822-852`) before `runProgrammaticAction`, resolve `input.binding` through the new ledger, snapshot the single dynamic route, dial via the existing raw executor on the session `dialRoute`, and push an `ObservedExchange` carrying the dynamic fields. Evaluate H1a's existing `/status` and `/body/status` pointers against a **runner-built** projection of the real response — which keeps H1a's authored assertions valid unchanged and satisfies item 6 without any parser, family-type or manifest edit. Then delete item 1's new-family requirement and say explicitly that the privilege is scoped by the private runner branch, not by a family name. Reuse `validateExactRunConfiguration` for the dynamic route — it is deliberately profile-free and standalone (`http-executor.ts:542-560`).
