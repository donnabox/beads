### 3 — LOW — the planner grants 4 MiB by self-declared `catalogId`, after parsing, to carriers its matching factory rejects

**Location** `packages/conformance/test-support/profile-contract-plan.ts:235-251`; compare `packages/conformance/src/artifact-bundle.ts:134` and `:186-187`.

The factory gates on an explicit parameter (`cumulative !== undefined`) and separately requires `manifestVersion === 2`. The planner gates on the selection only, then re-applies 1 MiB by reading `catalogId` **out of the already-parsed manifest**. Three consequences:

1. A non-cumulative carrier's bytes are UTF-8 decoded, `JSON.parse`d, complexity-walked and parsed into an `ExecutableScenarioManifest` at up to 4 MiB before the 1 MiB refusal fires — the byte cap exists to bound exactly that work. `read-update-w1b-repair-native-review.md:20` acknowledges this; `docs/design/read-update-executable-progress.md:91` does not.
2. A 2 MiB `{catalogId:"read-update-v1", manifestVersion:1, scenarios:[…]}` manifest is now **accepted** (plan returns with `carrierBlocker`) where before it threw on bytes. The matching factory refuses it at `artifact-bundle.ts:186`.
3. `:251` allocates and discards a full copy purely to throw.

**Fix** Minimally, add the version condition so the planner refuses what the factory refuses: `if (manifest && source && !(manifest.catalogId === "read-update-v1" && manifest.manifestVersion === 2)) copySourceBytes(…)`. Better: give `planProfileExecution` an explicit cumulative opt-in mirroring the factory's `cumulative` parameter, so the budget never depends on content read from the oversized bytes.

### 5 — LOW — every fresh-guard Link probe is a semantic no-op; the coverage reason says "retained usability"

**Location** all twelve `matching-link-*` actions (e.g. `packages/conformance/matrices/read-update-v1.json`, `owned-link-source-revision`/`matching-link-properties`); reasons at `read-update-coverage-v1.json:1406`, `:1914`, `:2362`.

**Fix** Disclosure, not new actions — adding a state-changing matching probe would perturb the staged revision chain. Append to the three reasons: "The matching Link guards are RFC 6902 §4.6 no-ops; they witness current-guard admission and revision retention, not admission for a state-changing Link write."
