## Findings

**1 — LOW · `apps/bdptest/src/installed-reference.test.ts:116-137` · failure-mode diagnosability**
Socket errors are pushed into `errors` and only asserted at `:149`, after the happy path. Neither `bounded(once(peer,"connect"))` nor `bounded(refusal)` races the error, so any connect refusal, RST or ECONNRESET stalls for the full 2 s and reports `"CONNECT peer connection deadline"` / `"CONNECT refusal arrival deadline"` with the actual errno silently discarded — the exact "fails as a deadline rather than as the property that broke" shape the prior review objected to in the probe. *Fix:* race the error (`once(peer,"error").then(([e]) => { throw e })`) inside each `bounded`, or assert `expect(errors).toEqual([])` immediately after the refusal await. The sibling observation loop already does this with `socket.once("error", reject)` (`observation.mjs:330`).

**2 — INFO · `installed-reference.test.ts:136` and `:141` · unfalsifiable assertion**
`peer.writableEnded` is set only by `peer.end()`; the server's FIN and its `socket.destroy()` cannot set it, and `allowHalfOpen: true` + `pause()` blocks the auto-end path. Both assertions therefore guard the test's own precondition against future edits (removing `allowHalfOpen`, adding a read) rather than pinning runtime behavior — which is not what `report.md`'s "verifies the client has sent no FIN before and after child shutdown" implies. *Fix:* keep one as a precondition guard and make the post-close one falsifiable: `expect(peer.destroyed).toBe(false)` (plus the existing `readableLength` check) actually demonstrates a live half-open socket at the moment the owner was released.

**3 — INFO · `installed-reference.test.ts:158` · budget headroom**
15 000 ms `it` budget against a nested worst case over 20 s: two `launchInstalledReference` calls at 10 s each (`observation.mjs:41-44`), two `requestInstalled` calls at 5 s each (`:118`), three 2 s bounds. Benign — observed 384-407 ms, and an overrun still fails with `afterEach` cleanup bounded — but the bad mode is an opaque vitest timeout replacing the specific message. Same class as prior Finding 5. *Fix (optional):* 30 000 ms, matching the test immediately above it.

**4 — INFO · evidence coverage, not code**
All measurements in both this pass and the prior review are darwin. CI is `ubuntu-latest` / Node 24.16.0 (`ci.yml:18,31`) and runs this file under `pnpm test` (`:66`). The unmeasured assumption is close-with-unread-data: the test's refusal-arrival await and `errors == []` both depend on the server's post-`end()` `destroy()` producing FIN rather than RST. Low risk — the server's receive queue is empty (the parser consumed the whole CONNECT request), and RST-on-close requires unread *inbound* data — but it is the one in-scope claim the frozen logs don't cover. *Fix:* nothing in code; just don't state cross-platform durability.
