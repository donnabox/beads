### Actionable Findings

#### Finding 1: Sequence turn stepper projects conflict/in-progress/terminal receipts without verifying sequence carrier cancellation safety
- **Severity**: Medium
- **File & Lines**: `packages/server/src/transaction-sequence.ts`: lines 36–63; `packages/server/src/transaction-store.ts`: lines 713–726
- **Concrete Issue**: In `transaction-sequence.ts`, when a sequence turn encounters an existing terminal failure (`turn.result.receipt.status === "failed"`), conflict (`kind === "conflict"`), or in-progress execution (`receipt.status === "pending"`), `project()` maps it to an entry problem and returns normally. However, in Read+Update and Transactional sequences, a failed member or conflict does not abort the remainder of the sequence in `nextSequence()`. If an earlier member encountered a fatal failure or an idempotency conflict, remaining sequence member reservations admitted in `admitSequence` remain durably reserved in SQLite with `owner` set until either all members step or the stepper is explicitly aborted. If a consumer stops iterating upon seeing an entry problem in `results` without calling `stepper.abort()`, those reserved pending receipts leak in SQLite until store restart.
- **Reachable Trigger**: A client submits a sequence via `createTransactionSequenceStepper` where member 0 fails business validation (e.g. 422 `validation-failed`) or encounters a 409 `idempotency-conflict`, and the caller stops calling `step()`.
- **Impact**: Uncommitted member keys reserved during `admitSequence()` remain locked in SQLite as pending receipts under the attempt's owner UUID until manual abort, crash, or restart recovery sweeps them.
- **Fix / Next Step**: In `createTransactionSequenceStepper`, if a step yields a terminal failure or conflict for which subsequent execution should halt or if the stepper is disposed, ensure remaining unexecuted reservations in `state.owned` are automatically retracted via `store.cancelSequence(admission)`.

#### Finding 2: `admitKey` direct singleton/batch route returns a pending receipt on semantic match instead of entering bounded comparison wait
- **Severity**: Medium
- **File & Lines**: `packages/server/src/transaction-store.ts`: lines 350–365; `docs/specs/bdp.md`: lines 3953–3969
- **Concrete Issue**: Under `docs/specs/bdp.md` lines 3953–3969, a direct singleton or batch carrier encountering an in-flight reservation MUST wait for comparison within a finite authority budget, or return `503 temporarily-unavailable` (`retry: after-delay`). When `admitKey` is called with `pendingWins = false` (the direct `store.admit()` route), line 352 skips the `pendingWins` branch, normalizes the transaction, and if `prior.identity === normalized.identity`, immediately returns `{ kind: "existing", receipt: parseReceipt(prior.receipt) }` where `prior.receipt.status === "pending"`. 
- **Reachable Trigger**: A concurrent identical direct singleton or batch request arrives while an admitted transaction is executing between `admit()` and `execute()`.
- **Impact**: The concurrent caller receives `{ kind: "existing", receipt: <pending> }` directly from `admit()`. While lines 3841–3844 permit handing a duplicate a pending receipt once semantic equality is proven, direct carriers exposing HTTP routes must not return raw internal pending ownership structures without coordinating response delivery or waiting for completion.
- **Fix / Next Step**: Document that carrier-level HTTP adapters wrapping `store.admit()` are responsible for managing the comparison wait loop or translating immediate pending receipt responses into the required transport representation (or awaiting execution completion within the comparison budget before answering).

#### Finding 3: `retract()` leaves claim in WeakMap state
- **Severity**: Low
- **File & Lines**: `packages/server/src/transaction-store.ts`: lines 746–758
- **Concrete Issue**: In `retract(claim: TransactionClaim)`, the pending receipt row is deleted from the SQLite `receipts` table (`DELETE FROM receipts WHERE principal=? AND key=? AND owner=?`), but `claims.delete(claim)` is never invoked.
- **Reachable Trigger**: Calling `store.retract(claim)` followed by a subsequent check or operation on `claim`.
- **Impact**: A subsequent call to `store.execute(claim)` will look up `claim` in `claims`, attempt to find the row in `receipts`, observe that `current` is missing, and throw `"transaction execution fenced"` rather than failing fast on `"foreign transaction claim"`. Calling `retract(claim)` a second time will attempt another redundant SQLite deletion and return `false`.
- **Fix / Next Step**: Add `claims.delete(claim);` inside `retract()` immediately after the database deletion succeeds.

### Readiness Summary

- **Durable Write Ownership & Admission**: Conforming. Single SQLite transaction for admission preserves the atomic `(principal, key)` invariant. Exclusive owner tokens (`randomUUID()`) prevent split-brain commits; crash recovery retracts orphan pending receipts on startup.
- **Idempotency & Retained Receipts**: Conforming. Semantic normalization accurately normalizes alias witnesses, protocol defaults, and batch creators. Expired completed receipts are compacted into immutable tombstones preserving allocated identities and positions; failed receipts are retained through `expiresAt` and cleanly forgotten without entering `detail: "expired"`.
- **Cumulative Boundaries**: Read -> Read+Update -> Transactional carrier layering is preserved without leaking synthetic RU keys into durable Transactional storage.
