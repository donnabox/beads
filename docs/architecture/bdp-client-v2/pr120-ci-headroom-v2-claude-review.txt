Conclusion unaffected; but this is the same defect class as prior required finding 1 — a plan claim contradicted by an artifact sitting beside it.

### F2 [Medium] The corpus's single best data point is uncited, and it is decisive

Job **115999221459** (`transaction-cold-schema-ci-first.json` / `-raw.log`), head **c555fe0** — same head and tree as the green 116007773987:

- `pnpm test` step **concluded `success` at 696 s**: `Test Files 172 passed (172)`, `Tests 9245 passed | 1 skipped (9246)`, `Duration 695.33s`.
- `e2e:ready:preflight` then ran and printed `Tests 7 passed (7)` / `Duration 1.50s` before the step was cancelled at ~3 s; smoke and the Read gate `skipped`.
- Setup 202 s. So the job needed ≈ **908 s** (202 + 696 + 3 + 5 + 1 + post) and had 900 s — **it missed green by single-digit seconds with the entire suite and the preflight already executed.**
- Same-head, same-tree whole-suite wall ratio: **695.33 / 409.53 = 1.698×**.

Three consequences for the plan:

1. Its central variance claim rests on matrix case-sum ratios across *different* heads (1.458, 1.721, 1.817) while a same-head, same-tree, **whole-suite** 1.70× measurement is on disk. That is strictly better evidence for required findings 1 and 2 and removes the "matrix ≠ suite" caveat the plan has to carry.
2. Line 34's "Current cancellation cannot supply a completed downstream measurement" is true of this run but incomplete at corpus level: the slow-regime downstream *is* measured (preflight 1.50 s Vitest / ~3 s step), corroborating the 30 s reserve as conservative against ~8–9 s of actual gate commands (green runs: 11 s and 12 s step-end→job-complete, verified).
3. It is the only evidence that the 15-minute cap can cut a run whose suite **completed** — precisely the failure mode 20 minutes removes.

Also uncited: **five of eighteen** retained `verify` jobs hit the 15 m cap — 39c160d/115911832057 (683 s), 8c78cba/115933355104 (697 s), 4aa2d0b/115984000843 (695 s), c555fe0/115999221459 (696 s), 427cfef/116044976221 (703 s). The plan quantifies two and alludes to "earlier retries". 28 % recurrence across one day of runs is a stronger recurrence argument than the one made.

### F3 [Low] "Extends outside the observed band" needs the word *aggregate*

Line 38 cites 1.817× and 1.721× as the observed episodes. The worst **aggregate** same-work ratio actually derivable is **1.838×** (current 56-case sum 658.206 s ÷ PR115-green same-56 358.064 s). More to the point, the prior review recorded a worst **single-case** ratio of 2.315×, which exceeds the corrected 2.25× tolerance at the worst observed setup. The aggregate claim holds (1.84× observed vs 2.25× tolerated); the sentence should say so explicitly rather than implying the whole band is cleared.

### F4 [Low] `cancel-in-progress` and a 20 m cap produce the same `cancelled` conclusion

`ci.yml:9-11` sets `cancel-in-progress: true` on `ci-${{ github.workflow }}-${{ github.ref }}`. A longer cap widens the window in which a concurrency cancel can land, and both surface as `conclusion: cancelled`. Gates 5/6 treat "cancellation" generically; they should require reading the annotation text (`"…maximum execution time of 20m0s"` vs its absence) to distinguish them, because the dispositions are opposite — diagnose/stop vs benign re-run. Attribution is sound for 427 (its annotation carries the 15m0s text); it is the *next* run that needs the rule.

### F5 [Low] The sizing baselines have a ~5-day validity window

The retained annotations carry: *"The ubuntu-latest label will migrate to Ubuntu 26 beginning October 19, 2026."* Every measurement in this corpus was taken Oct 13–14, 2026 on the pre-migration image, and the largest, most variable term — the uncached `go install` source build, 103–204 s — is exactly what a runner-image change moves. The plan correctly keeps `runs-on: ubuntu-latest` in scope-freeze, but the one subsequent exact-head attempt should land before the migration reaches this repo, and post-migration timings should be treated as a new baseline rather than compared to these.

### F6 [Low] Gate 3's parser branch is satisfiable — name the parser

In this worktree: no `yq`, no python `yaml` module, no `yaml`/`js-yaml` under `node_modules` (6 entries; workspace not installed). `/usr/bin/ruby` with Psych 3.1.0 **is** present and sufficient — `ruby -ryaml -e 'YAML.load_file(".github/workflows/ci.yml")'` resolves `jobs.verify.timeout-minutes` as a scalar Integer. Naming it keeps the author off the degraded text-inspection fallback without fetching a dependency. (I ran exactly this, read-only.)

- My ~715–720 s current-inventory estimate assumes the residual is the one missing case plus the summary. It does not prove per-file teardown or installed-child cleanup completed — that remains unobserved, as the diagnosis says, and no cleanup proof should be read out of the orphan-cleanup text.
