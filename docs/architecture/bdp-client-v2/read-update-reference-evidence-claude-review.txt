**Impact.** An authority that rejected *every* absolute-URI put target with `validation-failed` would pass `rb-alias-absolute`, `rb-link-absolute` **and** `rb-external`, plus every control in the row. The selected clause turns on category — "an alias, absolute or local, a Link, or an external URI" — and the external-URI category, one of the clause's three, sits entirely inside the hole. **Bounded fix.** One additional admitted put in row B with `target: "https://reference.invalid/acme/beads/rb-target"` → 200 plus its 307 alias readback. ≈40 nodes against 3,554 headroom; reuses the existing `alias-0` oracle kind; no runtime/runner/schema/catalog change.
